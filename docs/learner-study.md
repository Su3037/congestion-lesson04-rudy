# Learner study

**Status: not run.** It needs 3–5 real learners, which only the course author can recruit.

## Protocol (about 40 minutes per learner)

1. Pre-test: the 8-item concept quiz at `/study/` (phase "课前"). Each item targets one misconception.
2. The learner works through the lesson page and the lab on their own. Do not help.
3. Think-aloud (one learner is enough): ask them to say what they expect before pressing "Next" in the lab's step-through, then to explain the max-versus-total table in their own words. Note where they stall.
4. Post-test: the same quiz, phase "课后".
5. Send the CSVs; item-level results go in the table below, small n and all.
