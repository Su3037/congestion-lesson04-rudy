# Lab walkthrough · The RUDY lab

Narration transcript (matches the voice-over). Times match site/data/films/lab.clock.en.json.

## The lab

### Open the lab `w01` (00:00:00.000)

- This is the lab. It opens on the teacher's spread layout: a demand map with capacity 2.

### Pick a net `w02` (00:00:07.750)

- Click a net: its box and selected tiles light up, with each tile's share written inside.

## Change the layout

### Drag a pin `w03` (00:00:14.600)

- Drag a pin. Move A to x = 3 and the whole map is recomputed on the spot.

### Keyboard `w04` (00:00:21.350)

- Or use the keyboard: select a pin and press the arrow keys; Undo steps back at any time.

### Add nets one by one `w05` (00:00:28.500)

- Add nets one by one, and watch the demand accumulate.

### Capacity and layers `w06` (00:00:33.300)

- Switch layers to see ratio and overflow. Change capacity: demand stays, overflow moves.

## From the film

### Try this frame `w07` (00:00:41.050)

### Challenges `w08` (00:00:47.500)
